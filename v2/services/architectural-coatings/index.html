Architectural coatings on the Gold Coast

Architectural coating systems applied to the architect’s specification, from smooth contemporary facades to textured and stone-look finishes.

When an architect specifies a coating system, the finish is part of the design. We apply the named system to the manufacturer’s requirements, and match colour and texture to the approved sample.

Sample panels before full application: confirm this is offered.

Where we use it

  • Contemporary residential facades
  • Multi-residential and commercial buildings
  • Textured and stone-look facades
  • Design-led fitouts

What is in our scope

  • Substrate and render prep
  • Coating system applied to spec
  • Colour and texture matched to the approved sample
  • System documentation confirm

Systems

Dulux AcraTex, Rockcote, Unitex, Resene and Boral. If your spec names a system, we price and apply that system.

To price architectural coatings, send us:

  • Plans and elevationsWith the areas to be finished marked up.
  • The finish specSystem, colour and texture, if chosen.
  • Your programTarget start and how long you have.
  • Site accessScaffold, levels and who supplies what.
