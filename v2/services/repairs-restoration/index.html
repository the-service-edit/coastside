Repairs and restoration on the Gold Coast

Repairs to cracked, drummy or damaged render and plaster, matched to the existing finish. For builders on renovation jobs, building managers and homeowners.

Most repair work comes down to matching the texture, profile and colour of what is already there. We look at why it failed before we patch, so the repair lasts instead of cracking again.

Where we use it

  • Renovations and extensions
  • Strata and building maintenance
  • Pre-sale and pre-painting repairs
  • Heritage and older homes

What is in our scope

  • Inspection of the failed area
  • Removal of loose and drummy render
  • Repair to match the existing finish
  • Ready for paint or coating

Systems

Dulux AcraTex, Rockcote, Unitex, Resene and Boral. If your spec names a system, we price and apply that system.

To price repairs and restoration, send us:

  • Plans and elevationsWith the areas to be finished marked up.
  • The finish specSystem, colour and texture, if chosen.
  • Your programTarget start and how long you have.
  • Site accessScaffold, levels and who supplies what.
