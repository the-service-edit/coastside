Feature walls on the Gold Coast

Statement plaster and render feature walls for residential and commercial interiors, entries and outdoor areas.

A feature wall gets looked at up close, so the detail matters: clean edges, consistent texture and junctions that don’t catch the light. We work from the designer’s intent and the space it sits in.

Where we use it

  • Entries and foyers
  • Living areas and bedrooms
  • Restaurants, bars and retail
  • Outdoor and landscape walls

What is in our scope

  • Base preparation
  • Plaster, render or coating finish
  • Detailing to edges, niches and joinery
  • Sealing to spec

Systems

Dulux AcraTex, Rockcote, Unitex, Resene and Boral. If your spec names a system, we price and apply that system.

To price feature walls, send us:

  • Plans and elevationsWith the areas to be finished marked up.
  • The finish specSystem, colour and texture, if chosen.
  • Your programTarget start and how long you have.
  • Site accessScaffold, levels and who supplies what.
